Project Overview

This project explored how different prompting techniques can improve AI responses when working with real tasks.

Version 1 – Naive Prompt

I started with a simple request to summarize lecture notes. The result was accurate, but the prompt did not provide much context or detailed instructions.

Version 2 – Role Assignment

I assigned the AI the role of an experienced programming instructor and asked for a simple explanation suitable for a first-year student.

Version 3 – Context and Motivation

I added information about my level, the topic, and why I needed the summary. This made the response more focused on the important concepts.

Version 4 – Few-Shot Examples

I provided an example showing the style of answer I wanted. This helped the AI produce a more consistent and concise response.

Version 5 – Output Structure

I specified a clear structure including a title, summary, key concepts, comparison, and conclusion.

Version 6 – Step Decomposition

I broke the task into clear steps so the AI could organize the response more completely and reduce the chance of missing important information.

Results & Lessons Learned

The project showed me that adding role, context, examples, structure, and clear steps can make AI responses more useful and easier to understand.

Cross-Model Comparison

In my comparison, ChatGPT produced a clear and concise response, while Claude or Gemini provided slightly more detailed explanations. Both models completed the task successfully.
